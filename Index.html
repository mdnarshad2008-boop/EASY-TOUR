07 — Built for the hills

A few things that only matter here

Offline-first maps

Routes past Chopta and Kedarnath lose signal for hours. Maps, permits and your itinerary are cached before you set out.

Inner-line & eco-permits

Restricted zones near Kedarnath and Auli need a permit. Apply inside the app and carry a QR pass instead of paper.

Crowdsourced hazard reports

A tourist who sees a slip or a blocked road can flag it in one tap — it feeds the same AI guide, minutes later.

Local guides & artisans

Bookable directly, so more of what you spend stays in the hills.

Spread out, earn a badge

Choosing a quieter alternative over an overcrowded hotspot earns recognition — and eases pressure on the popular few.
